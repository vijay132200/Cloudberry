PHASE 1 — BEHAVIORAL CONSISTENCY SCORE CALCULATION FIX
======================================================

Current Problem:

Behavioral Consistency is currently calculated using the entire calendar week beginning on Sunday.

This creates inaccurate scores for newly enrolled patients because the system assumes data should exist before the patient joined the program.

Example:

Patient joins on Friday.

Current behavior:

The score calculation assumes the patient should have submitted data from Sunday through Friday.

Since no data exists before enrollment, the patient receives an artificially low Behavioral Consistency score.

This is incorrect.

==================================================
REQUIRED FIX
============

Behavioral Consistency must be calculated only from the patient's actual onboarding/enrollment date.

The calculation window must begin at:

patient.createdAt
or
patient.enrollmentDate
or
the actual onboarding date field used by the platform

Determine the correct enrollment source of truth and use it consistently.

Requirements:

* Days before enrollment must never be counted.
* Missing submissions before enrollment must never reduce the score.
* The denominator must dynamically adjust based on enrollment date.
* The score must only consider days where the patient was actually enrolled and expected to submit data.

Examples:

Example 1:

Enrollment Date:
Friday

Current Date:
Sunday

Expected denominator:
Friday, Saturday, Sunday only

NOT:

Sunday through Sunday of the calendar week

Example 2:

Enrollment Date:
Wednesday

Current Date:
Saturday

Expected denominator:
Wednesday through Saturday only
